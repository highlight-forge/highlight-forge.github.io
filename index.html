Stream highlights, cut for short-form video.

Highlight Forge spots the best moments from live game streams and turns them into vertical videos for clip accounts.

What Highlight Forge Studio does

  • Spots stand-out moments on public Twitch streams from how fast chat is moving.
  • Cuts the moment from the stream and reformats it to 9:16 vertical video.
  • Uploads the video to the clip account it was made for, as a draft or post in that account.

Creators

We only clip creators who have given permission. Each account is clearly labelled as a fan clip account and credits the original channel. If you're a creator and want a clip removed, or want us to stop clipping your channel, contact us and we will act on it promptly.
