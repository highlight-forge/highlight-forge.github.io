Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what data Highlight Forge Studio (“the app”), operated by Highlight Forge, handles and why.

What we collect

  • TikTok authorisation tokens and account ID for each account connected to the app. These are needed to upload videos to that account.
  • Upload records: which video went to which account, when, and the upload ID TikTok returned.
  • Public stream data: public Twitch chat messages and stream video, used only to find and cut clip moments. We don't build profiles of chat users, and we don't keep chat after a clip has been made.

What we don't collect

  • We don't read connected accounts' messages, followers, likes or any other private TikTok data.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts.

How it's stored

Tokens and upload records are stored only on the machine that runs the app, and they're never published or shared. We don't sell or rent data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Videos and their captions are sent to TikTok through its official API, to upload them to the connected account. Caption text may be generated with an AI service, which receives only the stream title and a short sample of public chat messages. Nothing else is shared.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. To disconnect, revoke the app in TikTok's settings under “Manage app permissions”. We then delete the stored token. You can also ask us to delete everything we hold about an account.

Children

The app isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly connect their accounts.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will show when it last changed.
